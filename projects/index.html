Work on the bench. Not products you can buy — things worth looking at while they are still unfinished.

Prototype

IOTransition

A bet that connected things only become ordinary after the awkward middle is cheap. The first product puts a normal household light switch on Wi-Fi, without turning the wall into a renovation.

  • Ordinary toggle, not a new smart plate you have to learn.
  • Meant to be inexpensive and almost effortless to install.
  • Prototype 1 is up, with a short video of the switch.

Research

Small bathroom vacuum

A Grok researcher is on this one. The room is the constraint: a vacuum that can work the strip beside the toilet, slide under the vanity toe-kick, and not panic because the floor is wet.

  • Small on purpose. A hallway robot does not fit this job.
  • The hard parts are clearance, the pedestal, and water on the floor.
  • Still in the reading, not a machine with a price.

The research bot is still working. Nothing public to open yet.

Building

Home server

A machine in the house for the work that should not live only on someone else’s computer. Sites, files, and the bench, without renting another box every month.

  • Quiet enough to leave running.
  • Meant to hold the other projects, not to be one you subscribe to.
  • Still being stood up. Nothing to log into.

No public address yet.

Gathering

Solar depot

A pile that wants to become a system. Panels, controllers, and leftover solar hardware, kept until they can put out power after the sun is gone.

  • Used and spare parts, not a catalog.
  • The interesting part is storage and use, not another brochure about panels.
  • No storefront and no install to book.

Nothing public to open yet.
