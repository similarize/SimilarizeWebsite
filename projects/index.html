Prototype

IOTransition

A bet that connected things only become ordinary after the awkward middle is cheap. The first product puts a normal household light switch on Wi-Fi, without turning the wall into a renovation.

  • Ordinary toggle, not a new smart plate you have to learn.
  • Meant to be inexpensive and almost effortless to install.
  • Prototype 1 is up, with a short video of the switch.

Research

Small bathroom vacuum

A Grok researcher is on this one. The room is the constraint: a vacuum that can work the strip beside the toilet, slide under the vanity toe-kick, and not panic because the floor is wet.

  • Small on purpose. A hallway robot does not fit this job.
  • The hard parts are clearance, the pedestal, and water on the floor.
  • Still in the reading, not a machine with a price.

The research bot is still working. Nothing public to open yet.

Queued

Home server

A personal mesh, not a Similarize product, and not started. Three separated machines — Murray, Magna, and a remote site — each on its own Starlink, so one site can keep working if the others go dark.

  • Local inference, files, a password vault, and an offline shelf. Nothing of it opened to the public internet.
  • Three PCs already on hand, about 32 GB each. No new cluster to buy on day one.
  • Tailscale between sites, because Starlink will not take inbound ports.

Researcher brief, 23 Sep 2026. Queued until Phase 1 is actually scheduled. Nothing to log into.

Solar depot

Two 30-foot trailers, unused batteries, and panels, waiting on land. Then they become mobile off-grid depots — tools, lights, and later the home-server nodes — without a utility hookup. This one owns power. The server project owns compute.

  • Stated storage is about 50 kWh: three ~10 kWh banks and one ~20 kWh. Nameplates still need a photograph before that is treated as fact.
  • Two depots first. The 20 kWh bank on one trailer, a 10 kWh bank on the other, one bank held spare.
  • Panel wattage is still unknown. Harvest before inverters.

Researcher brief, 23 Sep 2026. Queued until there is land. No storefront and no install.
