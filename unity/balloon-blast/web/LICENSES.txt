   (https://quaternius.com), via https://opengameart.org/content/stylized-nature-megakit.
   CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication). Decimated and converted for WebGL; textures resized. The fallen log
   uses the kit's bark texture.

   (Four Froggies frog design; see 4) - and James's robots, the same models as in Four Froggies (Unity):

   - "Unitree" = Unitree H1 robot model. Source: https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_mujoco (unitree_robots/h1),

4. Modelled for this game (no third-party mesh or texture): the airsoft rifle and gloved hands (no markings), the
   balloons, the Critters (four frogs, four cats, a German shepherd and a dachshund; the frog follows the Four Froggies
   frog design), the log fort, barn, sandbag nests, hay bales (generated straw textures), crates, pallets, barrels,
   tyres, the fallen log and its end-grain texture, the confetti / pop effects. Synthesised in the game at start-up:
   airsoft shots, balloon pops, hit-confirm ding, reload slide, jump, countdown beeps / GO, "your balloon popped" sting,
   wind.
