FIRE

Balloon Blast Airsoft

Everyone carries 3 balloons. Pop the others' balloons and keep yours. Last one with a balloon wins.

  • Keyboard and mouse: WASD to move, Shift to sprint, mouse to aim, click to shoot
  • Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick aims, RT or A shoots, Start restarts
  • Touch: left thumb moves, drag right side to aim, FIRE shoots
