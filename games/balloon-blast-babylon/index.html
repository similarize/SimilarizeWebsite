Balloon Blast Airsoft

Everyone carries 3 balloons. Pop the others' balloons and keep yours. Last one with a balloon wins.

  • Keyboard and mouse: WASD to move, Shift to sprint, mouse to look, left click to shoot, right click to aim, R to reload
  • Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick looks, RT or A shoots, LT aims, Start restarts
  • Touch: left thumb moves, drag right side to look, FIRE shoots, AIM toggles sights
