Balloon Blast Airsoft (Unity) - third-party assets and licences
================================================================

1. Sky + textures - Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), CC0 1.0 (public domain):
   - HDRI "Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)" by Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest (sky), tonemapped to a JPG.
   - Textures sparse_grass + leafy_grass (recoloured field grass), park_dirt (dirt patches), weathered_planks (crates,
     pallets, fort rails), weathered_plank_siding (barn, painted red), box_profile_metal_sheet (barn roof).
   https://polyhaven.com/license - "All assets on Poly Haven are licensed as CC0 ... You can use them for any purpose,
   including commercial work, without attribution." Credited anyway with thanks.

2. Trees, pines, bushes, rocks, grass tufts, ferns, flowers - "Stylized Nature MegaKit" by Quaternius
   (https://quaternius.com), via https://opengameart.org/content/stylized-nature-megakit.
   CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication). Decimated and converted for WebGL; textures resized. The fallen log
   uses the kit's bark texture.

3. Soldier figures - "X Bot" character (Mixamo, Adobe), as hosted in the Babylon.js asset library
   (https://assets.babylonjs.com/meshes/Xbot.glb), loaded at runtime and recoloured. Unchanged from earlier builds.

4. Modelled for this game (no third-party mesh or texture): the airsoft rifle and gloved hands (no markings), the
   balloons, the Critters (four frogs, four cats, a German shepherd and a dachshund; the frog follows the Four Froggies
   frog design), the log fort, barn, sandbag nests, hay bales (generated straw textures), crates, pallets, barrels,
   tyres, the fallen log and its end-grain texture, the confetti / pop effects. Synthesised in the game at start-up:
   airsoft shots, balloon pops, hit-confirm ding, reload slide, jump, countdown beeps / GO, "your balloon popped" sting,
   wind.

5. Sounds and music (all CC0 1.0 / public domain; trimmed, levelled and re-encoded as 4-bit ADPCM for the game):
   - Kenney (https://kenney.nl), CC0: "Impact Sounds" (footsteps on grass / wood, BB impacts on wood / metal / soft
     surfaces, landing), "RPG Audio" (magazine latch / click, strap), "Interface Sounds" (menu clicks, join, back,
     switch, select, open / close), "Music Jingles" (round start, round win, match win, round lost stingers).
   - Music, lobby: "Happy Clappy Loop" by OwlishMedia, https://opengameart.org/content/happy-clappy-loop (CC0).
   - Music, match: "Happy Beat" by burabotti, https://opengameart.org/content/happy-beat (CC0).
   - Cheers: "Shouts and Applauses of Teens #1" and "#2" by DenisChardonnet, BigSoundBank
     (https://bigsoundbank.com/shouts-and-applauses-of-teens-1-s0236.html, ...-2-s0237.html), CC0.
   - Applause: "Well Done" by qubodup, https://opengameart.org/content/well-done (CC0 since 2024-10-05).
   - "Cheers" by Nocturnal_Vanguard (AuraVoice), https://opengameart.org/content/cheers-0 (CC0).
   - Ambience: "Countryside #2" by Joseph Sardin, BigSoundBank (https://bigsoundbank.com/campaign-2-s0898.html), CC0,
     cut to a 36 s seamless loop.

CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode
